# Tikka monopod

Parametric, 3D-printable rear monopod that clamps to a Picatinny rail.
Everything is generated by `generate.py` (CadQuery) from `params.json`.

## Working on it

- Run: `venv\Scripts\python generate.py` (or `python generate.py other.json`).
  About 30 s with the clearance check on.
- Outputs (committed): `monopod_*.stl` in print orientation, plus
  `monopod_preview.png`. Regenerate and commit them with any geometry change.
- All lengths are in mm. Keys starting with `_` in `params.json` are notes.
  Unknown or missing keys stop the run.
- After a run, check:
  - no `ERROR:` lines (sanity checks in the generator);
  - `Checking clearances ... no overlaps` (moving parts checked at min, mid
    and max leg angle);
  - the Hardware block: every joint "through the nut";
  - the preview image.
- Coordinates: X along the rail (knob at -X), Y across (moving jaw at +Y),
  Z up, z = 0 = top of the base plate / roof.
- Keep scratch scripts out of the project folder.
- `fit_test.py [JAW_LIP values...]`: rail clamp test pieces, built from
  `params.json`, written to `fit_test/`.

## Design (current)

Knob → printed lead screw (along the rail) → slider → twin links → leg arm
→ pivoting sled foot.

- **Rail clamp:** fixed jaw is part of the body, moving jaw is separate.
  The clamp bolts run under the rail into nuts slid into side traps.
  Printed recoil lugs on the clamp floor sit in the rail cross slots and
  take recoil.
- **Body:** open-bottomed channel under the clamp. The screw journal goes
  through the knob end wall; the screw tip passes through the far wall,
  where a printed clip snapped into a groove stops the screw being pulled
  back out. Load on the foot pushes the slider away from the knob, so the
  knob face takes the thrust; the clip only holds the screw in place.
- **Stops:** fully retracted, the slider hits the far end wall. Fully
  extended, it runs off the thread onto the screw journal.
- **Pivots:** the leg pin's head and nut are recessed in the walls. The
  slider, link and foot pins run through a printed bushing that the bolt
  clamps solid, so plain nuts can be tightened.
- **Hardware:** 6 × M5×25 socket head bolts, washers and plain hex nuts.
  This is all the user has; keep every joint within `BOLT_LENGTH`.
- **Turning:** clockwise from the knob end = up (right-hand thread). The
  knob face is engraved "UP" with an arrow.
- **Printing:** body prints as assembled (channel down; the roof and the
  slider-pin slots bridge). Slider prints standing on end, screw knob-down,
  leg and links flat, foot deck-down. No supports.

Current numbers: rail to ground 90–127 mm, 12.9 knob turns end to end,
body 84 × 29 mm and 50 mm below the rail top, clamp 46 mm long.

- [x] Reworked all hardware for M5×25 + plain nuts: clamp bolts under the
  rail, recoil lugs, nut traps, recessed leg pin, printed bushings.
- [x] Strengthened the body: 5 mm under the leg pin and slot, 5 mm walls,
  4 mm roof, 2 mm+ clamp floor around the nut traps. Relinked
  (LINK_PIVOT 32, LINK_LENGTH 34, LEG_ANGLE_MIN 26).
- [x] Fixed the clamp bolt holes breaking out of the bottom of the moving
  jaw: the bolt now sits 1.5 mm of jaw above z = 0 (CLAMP_FLOOR 9 -> 10.6).
- [x] Added a printed retaining clip on the screw tip (`monopod_clip.stl`):
  before this the screw could slide about 3.5 mm back towards the knob.
- [x] Fixed the rail profile. The clamp had been modelled widest at the
  top face with a V-groove cut in. A real MIL-STD-1913 rail (and the
  user's Tikka T3x TACT A1 rear stock rail, confirmed by photo) is narrow
  at the top face, widens at 45 deg to `RAIL_WIDTH` 21.2, then narrows
  back to the neck (`RAIL_NECK_WIDTH` 15.6). The lips hook under the
  widest point by `JAW_LIP`. Test fit (`fit_test.py`): 1.5 fits best on
  the TACT A1 (of 1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5). Keep `JAW_LIP` at 1.5.
- [x] Recoil lugs now run right across the clamp floor, on the moving jaw
  as well as the fixed half, out to the walls.
- [x] Committed the linkage redesign, screw clip and rail fix (ac2b61c).
- [ ] First test print and fit on the rifle.

## Open questions / things to check on the first print

- The minimum height (90 mm) may be too tall; `LEG_LENGTH` trades minimum
  height against range.
- Adjustment is coarse near retracted (~6.5 mm per turn); `SCREW_PITCH`
  2.5 would make it finer.
- The ~45 mm wall-slot bridges in the body may sag.
- The clamp and leg-pin bolts only clear their nuts by 0.5 mm. Washers
  thicker than 1 mm need `WASHER_THICKNESS` changed and a rerun.
- The "UP" arrow direction was worked out from the thread geometry but
  has not been checked visually.
- Clip snap fit: `CLIP_SNAP` (0.5) sets the grip. Raise it if the clip
  falls off, lower it if the clip won't go on.
- The rail clamp length the rifle can take has not been confirmed (the
  clamp is 46 mm).
